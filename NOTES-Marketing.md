**How open source fits the business**

The repository identifies its license as MIT. That license permits commercial use and selling copies, subject to its notice requirements. Customers can also use the code themselves, so your paid offering needs value beyond access to the source. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

Your commercial advantage can come from reliable operation, easy onboarding, original content, expertise, and customer relationships. Before selling content packs, verify commercial and redistribution rights for every included model, animation, texture, and audio asset.
