# v0.19.38 — ThreeD Objects: Moving + Placement

Status: released to production, deployment User-confirmed. User reviewed and accepted the development checkpoint. Package 0.19.38.

## Goal and proved problem

Assigned Plantings must remain inside their Project Bed and visibly rooted at its top surface. Selecting a Bed should show its assigned Planting count and provide a compact way to select/focus those Plantings.

`src/app/api/project/threed-markers/route.ts` checks assigned Bed existence on Planting creation, but adds quantity offsets to independent user X/Y/Z values without testing Bed bounds. `src/libraries/services/threed/plantings/project-planting-placement-core.ts` bounds coordinates numerically rather than relative to a Bed. `src/components/threed/markers/BedMarker3D.tsx` renders the Bed body from its base position to base Y + height, with a border above the soil. `src/components/map/details/DetailsCard.tsx` hosts the existing Bed instance editor and is the appropriate location for assigned-Planting discovery.

## Acceptance criteria

Assigned Planting root positions are within the rendered usable Bed footprint and at its planting surface after placement, editing, Bed transforms, explicit save and reload. Procedural and Model-backed Plantings remain visible from above. Spacing does not collapse into overlapping rows. Bed counts and lists reflect actual active assignments and select the correct Project Planting. Unassigned Plantings and unrelated Projects retain existing behavior.

Containment concerns the planting root/anchor; foliage may extend beyond a Bed edge. The surface is the soil/top body rather than the raised border. Exact clearance and margin require renderer inspection and browser verification before implementation.
