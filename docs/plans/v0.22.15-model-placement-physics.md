# v0.22.15 — ThreeD Models: preserve placement at rest

## Prove — October 3, 2026

The Developer approved review of existing Model physics followed by a focused Rapier improvement. Existing Ecctrl Model controls are Developer-reported solid and must be preserved.

Inspection of `SceneMarkerRigidBody` in `src/components/map/ThreeDScene.tsx` found that its before-physics-step callback processes explicit Model placement first, resets dynamic velocities and clears pending transforms, then processes a queued completed Soccer kick. The kick guard rejects pending prop transforms but does not reject a placement consumed earlier in that step. An in-range kick can therefore give the deliberately repositioned ball fresh velocity immediately after it was placed at rest. This is a source-level transaction conflict; no browser occurrence is claimed.

Acceptance: explicit Model placement wins over a queued kick in the same physics step. Placement retains exact position and zero linear/angular velocity before simulation; the superseded kick reports `applied: false` once and is consumed. Ordinary valid kicks in later steps still apply; pending prop transforms, hidden bodies and foreign identities retain their established guards. Preserve fixed Model placement, unrelated bodies, Character animation completion rules, Ecctrl controls, stable marker ownership and the persistent Canvas/Rapier world.

Affected files: Scene wrapper, a focused offline actual-callback/Rapier regression and its validation dispatcher registration, package/lockfile root versions, AGENTS.md current-package annotation, CONTEXT.md, validation documentation and this plan. No schema, persistence expansion, live database/storage, restoration, cleanup, physics tuning or restructuring. Stage 3A remains partially verified and paused. Initial worktree is clean.
