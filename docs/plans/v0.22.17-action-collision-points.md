## Implementation

The reusable observation contract lives in `action-collision-core.ts`: request/Project identity, actor/target marker IDs, semantic Action, clip name, point/source node, world-space segment and radius. Default supported foot-kick Actions select left/right foot/toe points from the actual loaded Farmer/Mixamo rig. The sampler requires a varying transform track on the point or its skeletal ancestors and observes Three.js world transforms after the existing mixer/facing updates. Ambiguous/missing bindings and unrelated/stationary clips report unavailable. Sweeps over two Scene units or frames longer than 100 ms are discarded, and the next pose primes a fresh observation. Shoe tolerance scales from the foot/toe separation within 0.05–0.3 Scene feet; ankle-only fallback uses 0.15 feet. This is a bounded rig-point approximation, not full skinned-mesh collision.

Ecctrl arms sampling only during the requested kick clip, after any outbound turn; it clears before return/locomotion and on release, hiding, model replacement and cleanup. First observations and final held poses are excluded. Contact that exists only at the terminal held pose may therefore miss; Project 16's actual kick-clip timing/alignment must be verified in the browser. Shared sampling does not manipulate the mixer, create foot bodies or write world state.

Scene correlates the exact request/Project/actor/ball, Action/clip and allowed point, then forwards a bounded sample queue to the existing ball owner. The next safe Rapier step checks the enabled non-sensor sphere collider's actual world center/radius, including its local offset, and rechecks control, layer/visibility, identity, live range and placement. A swept hit applies one existing bounded impulse; misses continue sampling and animation completion reports a miss without a fallback impulse. Request identity prevents repeats. Explicit placement cancels contact requests and still has priority over queued assisted kicks.

DetailsCard foot-kick Actions use contact timing when an exact eligible movable ball is targeted. Without that target they remain previews; header/pass/penalty stay previews. **Kick selected ball** retains its completion-timed assisted behavior. Tooltips and result text distinguish contact, miss, unsupported collision points and cancellation. The UI releases its pending request after contact results; a second click while the first clip finishes is safely rejected by the existing Character task lock.

This is the first default consumer of a shared collision-point contract. Arbitrary mesh-point authoring, persisted collision profiles and additional Action effects are future work; this change grants no general world-mutation authority to mesh contact. Ordinary walking push strength and saved physics settings are unchanged.
