## Project header menus - November 17 follow-up

The Developer reported Title/Add menus sitting too low and two unwanted scroll areas in Scene after installing Radix. Read-only inspection now verifies active Radix 1.8.1. This is a scoped browser defect report, not general acceptance of the release.

Reproduction identified existing App layout causes: Dashboard's backdrop-filter container offsets fixed descendants while useToolbarMenuBounds supplied viewport coordinates, adding the 48px App Header twice. The active Scene menu is custom markup, not a Radix DropdownMenu: its 32rem cap plus max-height/overflow on both Saved Views and Scene Layers produced nested scrolling. The before-fix native fixture measured Title/Add at y=134 against toolbar bottom y=86, two nested Scene scroll owners, and inaccessible lower content on a short viewport.

The shared hook now converts viewport bounds to the actual absolute positioning parent's coordinates. Title/Add retain their existing alignment, and Scene uses the same bounds with right alignment. Scene expands into the available viewport height; Saved Views and Scene Layers flow within its one outer scroll area. No menu actions, settings, ordering, save/load, layer ownership or Canvas/Rapier lifetime changed. The retained ThreeDDesign renderer was not edited; its shared list components also inherit the removal of nested scrolling.
