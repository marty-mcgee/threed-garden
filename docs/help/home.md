# ThreeD Home help

## Create and explore

Draw a room, arrange a garden layout, or explore a Scene with Models and Characters. Begin with a small Project and inspect it from different views. [Learn ThreeD](https://threedgarden.com/learn) explains how to create, edit, save and reopen your work.

## Garden demo

The illustrative Home garden lets you practice orbit and zoom without an account. Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. It does not support drawing or saving. To draw and save, sign in and open your own Project from Dashboard.

## Choose a destination

- ThreeD Garden opens the linked Project Scene, with Models, Characters, physics and maps. FarmBot integration has separate setup and authority requirements.
- Traffic Monitor shows CHP, Caltrans and Calfire incident information.
- Music Streaming opens the Multimedia library to browse Albums and listen to Tracks.
- Open-Source Project opens the source and developer documentation on GitHub. ThreeD Garden uses the MIT License.

The Home page also retains its interactive garden, technology links and featured Multimedia player when an Album and Track are available.
