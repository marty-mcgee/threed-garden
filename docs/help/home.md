# ThreeD Home help

## Create and explore

Draw a room, arrange a garden layout, or explore a Scene with Models and Characters. Begin with a small Project and inspect it from different views. [Learn ThreeD](https://threedgarden.com/learn) introduces the tools; the [Dashboard guide](../users/DASHBOARD_GUIDE.md) explains the Project workflow.

## Garden demo

The illustrative Home garden lets you practice orbit and zoom without an account. Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. It does not support drawing or saving. To retain your own work, create a Project and use **Save ThreeD Project** in its Project menu or the toolbar Save icon.

## Sign in and start your own Project

1. Open **Sign in** at `/auth/sign-in`. Enter **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Sign In**. The password visibility control lets you check your entry.
2. If you need an account, choose **Create an account**, complete the signup form and select **Sign Up**. New accounts are Admins of their own content; Super Admin access is managed separately.
3. After signing in, Admin opens at `/admin`. Choose **Projects > Add Project**, enter a Project Name and review access/measurement preferences, then **Create Project**.
4. Configure **Modules and assets**, then use **Open Project Scene** to begin. Choose **Save ThreeD Project** in the Project menu or the toolbar Save icon, then reopen your Project to check the saved result.

If signup reports that the account was created but sign-in failed, use the form's **Sign in** link rather than creating the same account again. See the [Admin guide](../users/ADMIN_GUIDE.md) for Project setup, account management and reusable content.

## Choose a destination

- **ThreeD Garden** opens the linked Project Scene. It is a destination for exploration; create your own Project to save your own edits. FarmBot integration has separate setup and authority requirements.
- **Traffic Monitor** opens `/dashboard/traffic` for CHP, Caltrans, CalFire and other available traffic sources.
- **Music Streaming** opens `/dashboard/multimedia` to browse public Albums and play available Tracks.
- **Open-Source Project** opens the source and developer documentation on GitHub. ThreeD Garden uses the MIT License.

## Listen and read Lyrics

When a featured Album and Track are available, Home shows the Multimedia player. Use its playback controls and open **Lyrics** to read eligible active Lyrics assigned to the Album or its active Tracks. Assignment labels identify the Album/Tracks, and Lyrics linked in several ways appear once. A missing Lyrics entry can mean no eligible text is assigned.

Choose **Full Library** to browse more Albums in Dashboard Multimedia. Publishing and editing Albums, Tracks and Lyrics happens in Admin; see [Add and display Lyrics](../users/ADMIN_GUIDE.md#add-and-display-lyrics).

Use [App Settings](../users/SETTINGS.md) to choose personal appearance and navigation preferences. Home also links to the technologies used by the App and the source repository.
